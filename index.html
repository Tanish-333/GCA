Our Mission & Heritage

Nurturing Gujarati Heritage in Waterloo Region

The Gujarati Cultural Association (GCA) of Golden Triangle is a non-profit community organization dedicated to preserving and celebrating the rich culture, traditions, and language of Gujarat across Kitchener, Waterloo, Cambridge, and surrounding townships.
